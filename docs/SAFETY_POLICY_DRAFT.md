# Safety Policy — Draft

## Product commitments

Spectrum will provide visible block, report, and unmatch controls; exclude blocked users from discovery; limit sensitive-data exposure; keep exact location private; and maintain trained moderation and appeals workflows before public launch.

## Safer dating guidance

- Keep early conversations in the app.
- Never send money or financial credentials.
- Meet first in a public place that fits both people’s sensory needs.
- Share plans with a trusted person and arrange your own transportation.
- Discuss touch, communication, timing, and exit plans directly.
- Leave if consent, boundaries, or safety feel uncertain.

## Reports

Report harassment, scams, fake profiles, inappropriate content, hate/discrimination, unsafe behavior, or another concern. Reports are confidential from the reported member. Moderators may review relevant account, profile, and conversation context and preserve necessary evidence.

## Crisis limitations

Spectrum is not an emergency, crisis, medical, or law-enforcement service. Contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis provider for immediate danger or urgent support. Region-specific resources must be reviewed and added before launch.
