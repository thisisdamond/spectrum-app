# Community Guidelines — Draft

Spectrum is for clear, kind, consensual connection.

## Expected

- Say what you mean respectfully and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect response time, sensory limits, boundaries, pronouns, and the right to stop contact.
- Use current, accurate photos and honest relationship intentions.
- Obtain explicit consent before sexual messages, physical contact, recording, or sharing private content.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats, stalking, coercion, hate, discrimination, fetishization, or ridicule.
- Targeting, manipulating, or financially exploiting autistic or otherwise vulnerable people.
- Impersonation, scams, spam, scraping, block evasion, or coordinated abuse.
- Non-consensual intimate content, sexual content involving minors, or illegal activity.

Use in-app block and report tools. Immediate danger should be reported to local emergency services. Enforcement may include warnings, content removal, reduced visibility, suspension, termination, and referral where legally required.
