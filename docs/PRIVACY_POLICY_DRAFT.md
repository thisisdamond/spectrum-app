# Spectrum Privacy Policy — Draft

**Status:** Product draft for qualified privacy-counsel review. **Effective date:** To be set before launch.

Spectrum is designed for adults seeking connection with clearer communication and sensory-aware preferences. We collect only the information needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

## Information we collect

- Account data: email, authentication records, age-eligibility status, and security settings.
- Profile data you choose to provide: display name, photos, prompts, interests, dating preferences, communication and sensory preferences, and approximate location.
- Activity: likes, passes, matches, blocks, reports, messages, notification choices, and feature interactions.
- Device and security data: app version, device type, push token, IP address, crash diagnostics, and abuse-prevention signals.
- Subscription data: product, entitlement, and transaction identifiers from the app store/payment provider. Spectrum does not store full payment-card details.

Autism-related or neurodivergence-related self-descriptions are optional. Spectrum does not require proof of diagnosis. Do not add medical records to your profile or messages.

## How we use information

We use data to provide authentication, profiles, matching, messaging, notifications, subscriptions, customer support, moderation, fraud prevention, accessibility, safety, legal compliance, and service improvement. Compatibility uses user-controlled preferences; it does not score autism “severity.”

## Visibility and sharing

Public profile fields are shown to eligible members. Email, authentication data, exact location, blocks, reports, and internal moderation notes are not public. Messages are shared with their participants and may be reviewed when reported or where required for safety or law.

We use service providers for hosting, storage, analytics, notifications, email, moderation, and subscriptions under contracts that limit their use of data. We do not sell personal information. A final policy must include any legally required “share” or targeted-ad disclosures based on the actual analytics stack.

## Retention, deletion, and export

We retain information while an account is active and as reasonably necessary for safety, disputes, legal obligations, and fraud prevention. Members may request a portable export and account deletion. Some limited records—such as block, payment, abuse, and legal records—may be retained where necessary and permitted.

## Security

We use transport encryption, password hashing, restricted access, private media storage, logging, and security monitoring. No system is perfectly secure. Members should report suspected compromise promptly.

## Rights and choices

Depending on location, members may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, withdraw consent, or obtain a copy of personal data. Settings control discovery, notifications, message previews, profile fields, and accessibility preferences.

## Age, international transfers, and contact

Spectrum is for people age 18 and older. Production terms must identify the operating company, privacy contact, representative/DPO where required, legal bases, international-transfer safeguards, region-specific notices, and regulator complaint rights.

We will provide notice of material changes before they take effect.
